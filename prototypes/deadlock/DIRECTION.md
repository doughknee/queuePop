# Street-poster direction

The supplied reference images are the visual target. The earlier bureau pass was too green, evenly lit, rounded, and refined. Adding subtle texture did not correct its composition or type.

Palette: soot #0b0c0c, charcoal #202525, dirty cream #eadfc6, ash #b4b4aa, rust #be7650, spectral turquoise #85cab5. Charcoal/black occupy the majority of every view; cream is used for labels and small paper scraps. Turquoise belongs to selected states and supernatural accents.

Type: Germania One for substantial main headings and character names, Barlow Condensed Bold for controls and poster strips, Arial for descriptions. Both display fonts are locally served, open-licensed alternatives; no claim of being the game's proprietary fonts.

Layout: city scene and oversized torn character posters on the homepage; persistent readable navigation. Archive pages are ink-on-dark with isolated paper notes and large illustrated portraits. Keep the established full-page routes and functional content.
